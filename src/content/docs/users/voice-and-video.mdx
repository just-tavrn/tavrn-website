Voice in tavrn is always end-to-end encrypted: the node passes the encrypted audio and video on, but cannot listen
or watch. Because of that, there are no recordings on the server.

## Joining a voice channel

Click a voice channel to join it. The channel list shows who is in which channel, with icons for muted, deafened,
camera and screen sharing. While you are connected, the **voice bar** at the bottom of the sidebar keeps the
buttons to mute, deafen and leave at hand, and shows the connection quality, also while you read text channels.

<Screenshot scene="voice-desktop" />

If your connection drops, tavrn reconnects by itself; the others see a small plug on your avatar meanwhile.

To switch to another voice channel, click it, or drag your own name from the channel list onto it. A channel you
may not join shows a red outline and refuses the drop.

The header of a voice channel shows how long the current session has been running, the same for everyone, also
for those who join late, and a **status** line under the channel's name, such as "Raid at nine". Members with the
permission *Set voice channel status* click the line (or its pencil) to change it: Enter saves, Escape cancels. The
channel list shows both when you point at a voice channel.

## Microphone, push-to-talk and noise suppression

Everything is under **Settings → Voice & video**, saved for each device:

- **Input mode**: voice activation, or push-to-talk with a key you choose. In the browser the key works while the
  tab has the focus; in the desktop app it also works while a game is in front.
- **Noise suppression**: off, your browser's own, or a noise filter that runs on your device. Echo cancellation,
  automatic gain and a noise gate are there too.
- **Tests**: "Hear yourself" plays your microphone back as others hear it, "Record and play back" records five
  seconds.
- **Remind me when I talk while muted** (on by default): when you speak while your microphone is muted, tavrn plays
  a soft double tap, the microphone button pulses and a short notice says so. Nothing is sent while you are muted;
  your microphone is only listened to on your device. A released push-to-talk key does not count as muted.
- **Notification sounds**: the sounds for people joining and leaving, mute, deafen, streams and the connection, each
  with its own switch and a button to play it, and their volume.

A right-click on the microphone or headphones button opens a quick menu with your devices and volume sliders. You
can also set the volume of each person from their menu.

**Mute for you**, in a person's menu, silences their voice and their stream on your device only; nobody is told.
Their tile and their entry in the member list show a crossed speaker (a click on it lifts the mute), and the call
header counts how many people you muted this way. Reloading the page makes everybody audible again.

## Camera and screen sharing

Turn the camera on from the call controls; the settings offer background blur or an image behind you. On phones
and tablets, a long press on the camera button switches between the front and the back camera.

**Share screen** first asks for the resolution, the frame rate and the sound, then your browser asks which screen,
window or tab to share. The node decides the highest resolution and frame rate. For the sound you choose **No
sound** or **Whole system** (everything your computer plays); a line under the choice says what works where you
are. Chrome and Edge 141 or newer on Windows and macOS, and the Windows app, leave the call itself out of the shared
sound, so nobody hears themselves. Where that is not possible, sharing starts without sound and warns you: use
headphones if you turn it on. Firefox, Safari, the Linux app and the macOS app share no sound with a screen; the
Android app can share the sound of other apps.

Nobody receives a stream until they join it: a shared screen shows as a tile with the name and **Join stream**, and
**Leave stream** stops watching again. The whiteboard is a tile of its own in the same way.

## Soundboard

Rooms can have a soundboard: short clips that members with the permission play into the call from the call
controls. Room owners add clips under **Room settings → Soundboard**.

## Calls in direct messages

The call button in the header of a direct message or group rings the others on all their devices, also on other
nodes. A call nobody answers within 45 seconds is listed as a missed call.

:::tip
If others cannot hear you, run **Settings → Voice & video → Test connection**. It tries every way to reach the
voice server and tells you what to fix. [Troubleshooting](/docs/users/troubleshooting/) has more.
:::
