## Formatting

Messages understand Markdown: `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `` `code` ``, quotes starting with `>`, lists and links.
Code blocks between three backticks get a copy button. To hide a spoiler, write `||like this||` or select the text
and press Ctrl+Shift+S; readers reveal it with a click. Attachments can be marked as spoilers too.

## Replies, reactions and emojis

- **Reply** quotes the message you answer.
- **React** opens the emoji picker with quick reactions, recently used emojis, the room's own emojis and your
  personal emojis. Custom emojis can also be written as `:name:`.
- **Edit** and **Delete** work on your own messages; edited messages are marked "(edited)".

<Screenshot scene="emoji-picker-desktop" />

Emojis in a message are a little larger than the text, and a message of only one to three emojis shows them at
twice the size. Country flags show as flags everywhere, also on Windows, whose own emoji font has none.

Mention people with `@name` (or `@name@domain` for someone on another node), a whole role with `@Role`, or
everyone with `@everyone` where you may. Mentions notify the people you name. In the message they show as small
highlighted labels; a mention of you, `@everyone` and `@here` stand out in a warning colour, and the whole message
that mentions you is tinted.

## Polls

Ask a question with a **poll**: choose **＋** next to the message box and then **Poll** or **Date poll**, or type
`/poll` followed by the question. Polls work in text, announcement and forum channels, in threads and in direct
messages. In a room they need the permission *Create polls*, which everyone who may write has unless the room
changes it.

- A **poll** has 2 to 20 options, with a single answer or "Allow several answers".
- A **date poll** finds a date: pick days in the calendar, each one all day or with times, and everybody answers
  each of them with **Yes**, **Maybe** or **No**. Times are shown in each reader's own time zone.
- **Show results** always, only after voting, or only when the poll closes; **votes** public (who voted for what)
  or hidden until the poll closes; and when the poll **closes**: never, after an hour, a day, a week, or at a time
  you pick.

Vote with the buttons in the message; **Change vote** and **Retract vote** work until the poll closes. Results show
bars with the number of votes, and a date poll puts the best dates on top, with **Show all answers** for the full
grid. The author, or a moderator with *Manage messages*, can **Close poll now**. A poll cannot be edited after it is
sent.

<Screenshot scene="poll-desktop" />

Once you voted, the poll shows its results with your own vote marked, and **Change vote** opens your answers again.
The results of a date poll stay small with many dates: a grid with the days as columns and the times as rows, a
star on the best dates. Choose a date in the grid to see who answered what.

<Screenshot scene="poll-date-desktop" />

<Screenshot scene="poll-phone" />

In an encrypted conversation the question, the options and every vote are end-to-end encrypted; the node only sees
that somebody voted and when the poll closes. "Hidden" results and votes are a promise of the apps, not anonymity:
every member's app can read all votes, and the poll composer says so.

## Buttons, menus and forms from bots

Bots can put **buttons** and **select menus** under their messages, and answer a click with a small **form**. The
app draws them with its own controls, so they look and work like the rest of tavrn on every screen; the bot only
chooses the words, an emoji and one of a few styles. What a bot answers can be a new message, a changed message, a
short notice under the message that **only you can see**, or a form, which opens as a dialog with the bot's name.

<Screenshot scene="components-desktop" />

A link button always shows you the full address before it opens anything. In an encrypted channel, what you choose
or type into a form is encrypted for the channel's members like a message. Room owners decide who may use these
controls with the permission *Use buttons and menus of apps*.

<Screenshot scene="components-modal-desktop" />

## Pins and threads

Moderators **pin** important messages; the channel header lists them under **Pinned messages**.

A **thread** is a side conversation about one message. Choose **Create thread** or **Reply in thread** in the
message's menu. The thread opens next to the channel (full screen on a phone), and the thread button in the
channel header lists the active and archived threads. Writing in a thread follows it, so you are notified about
new replies there.

## Attachments

Drag files into the channel or use the attach button. Your node sets the largest size and the allowed file types.
Before an image is uploaded, tavrn removes hidden information such as the place where a photo was taken. Images
from strangers wait behind a **Load** button, depending on your setting under **Settings → Privacy & safety → Image
previews**. If the node offers it, the composer also has a GIF search.

In encrypted channels, attachments are encrypted on your device like the messages themselves. Images open in a
viewer with zoom and a download button.

## Slash commands

Type `/` at the start of a message to open the command list; `/help` shows every command you can use where you
are. A few examples:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/me` | Sends an action in the third person |
| `/shrug` | Appends a shrugging emoticon to your message |
| `/nick` | Sets your nickname in this room |
| `/poll` | Opens the poll composer with your question |
| `/whiteboard` | Opens the shared whiteboard of the conversation |
| `/timeout`, `/kick`, `/ban` | Moderation commands, for members with the permission |

Bots add their own commands, which appear in the same list.
