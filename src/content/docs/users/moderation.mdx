As the owner of a room you decide who may do what. Everything below is in **Room settings**, which you open from
the room's name at the top of the sidebar.

## Roles and permissions

Under **Roles** you create roles such as "Moderator", give them a colour, choose whether they appear separately in
the member list and whether people may mention them, and switch their permissions on or off. Roles higher in the
list weigh more. You can only grant permissions you have yourself, and `@everyone`, the role every member has, can
never get management permissions.

<Screenshot scene="roles-desktop" />

## Category and channel permissions

Permissions come in layers. Roles give the base. A **category** can change a role's permissions for all its
channels, and a **channel** can change them again for itself; the channel wins. A switch left at "–" takes the
value from the layer below, which is shown in grey. **Reset to category** removes a channel's own changes. Members
only see the channels they may view.

## Viewing the room as a role

To check what a role really gets, choose **Roles → View room as this role**. The room then looks the way it looks
for a member with only that role (or only `@everyone`; you can add further roles and a join date): the channels
they see, what they may do in each, the menus and the settings. A banner "Viewing as …" with **Exit preview** stays
on every page of the room. The preview runs only on your device and changes nothing: sending, voice, uploads and
every other change are switched off while it lasts. In encrypted channels the history a new member with that role
would not get is cut off at "History for this role starts here".

## Keeping order

Open a member's menu to remove (kick), ban or time out someone, mute them in voice, or move them to another voice
channel. Timeouts last from minutes up to 28 days. You cannot act against someone whose highest role is not below
yours. **Bans** can name a single address or a whole domain, with a reason.

**Slowmode** limits how often each member may write in a channel. **Bulk delete** removes many messages at once
after showing a preview.

## Reports

Members report messages, people or a whole room from the menus. Members with the permission to moderate members
find the reports under **Reports**, where they assign, resolve or dismiss them and act directly. The reported
person is never told who reported them, but reports are not anonymous to moderators.

Nobody but the members can read an encrypted message. The person who reports it may choose to share its text, and
moderators see it marked as "released by the reporter – not verified".

<Screenshot scene="moderation-desktop" />

Node admins see every report of their node under **Administration → Reports**. When a report is about someone with
an account on another node, only that node's admins can act on the account, so the admin can **forward** the report
to the person's home node. Before anything is sent, a confirmation says what leaves the node: the category, the
reporter's text and the reported message. The reporter's address does not; the other node only sees a pseudonym.
Reports that other nodes forwarded are marked with the node they came from.

<Screenshot scene="admin-reports-desktop" />

## Auto-moderation

**Auto-moderation** blocks or removes messages with words or links you list, with exceptions for roles and
channels. It only works in channels without encryption, because the node cannot read encrypted ones. The node's
own protection against message floods applies everywhere.

<Screenshot scene="room-automod-desktop" />

## The audit log

The **Audit log** records what moderators and admins did, with their reasons. It helps a team see what happened
and why.

## Handing over a room

**Room settings → Overview → Transfer ownership** gives the room to another member of the same node. The overview
also sets how long messages are kept, and members who reset their security key can ask you to restore their
encrypted history under **History requests**.
