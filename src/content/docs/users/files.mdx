A **files channel** is a shared folder for a room. It works like a file browser: folders, a list or grid view,
sorting, previews of pictures and videos, and uploads with a button or by dragging files in.

<Screenshot scene="files-desktop" />

## Working with files

Open a file's menu (right-click, **⋯**, or press and hold) to download, rename, move or delete it. Folders work the
same way. Downloads only start when you choose the download icon, and SVG images are never opened in the app,
only downloaded, because they can contain code.

How much a channel can hold is set by the room and the node. Members need the permission to attach files to upload.

## Encrypted files channels

New files channels are end-to-end encrypted unless the person who creates them unticks it. Files are encrypted on
your device before they leave it, together with their names and folder names. The node stores only encrypted data
and sizes; it cannot see what the files are called or what they contain.

Because the node cannot read the names, the search box filters only the open folder. The search panel of the app
still finds files of encrypted channels through the search index on your device. A channel's encryption is fixed:
to change it, create a new files channel and upload the files again.

Files channels without encryption show a permanent banner saying that their files are not end-to-end encrypted.
A room can also keep such a channel on an external storage of its own.

## Your own uploads

**Your files** in the toolbar lists everything you uploaded to the channel, with sizes and dates. You can delete
your uploads there even when this device cannot decrypt them, so lost keys never leave your files on the node.

## Attachments in messages

Files you attach to messages belong to the message, not to a files channel. A node or a room may delete attachments
after a number of days; the message then shows "file expired". Files of pinned messages are kept.
