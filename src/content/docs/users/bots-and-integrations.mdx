A **bot** is an account that belongs to a person, its owner. Bots carry a **Bot** badge everywhere, so you always
know when you talk to one. They can answer commands, post messages and help with moderation.

## What a bot can see

In an encrypted channel, a bot reads and writes only as a regular member of the channel's encryption, just like a
person's device. It can read every encrypted channel it can view, and nothing else. When a bot joins or leaves an
encrypted channel, the channel shows a notice to everyone.

## Adding a bot to a room

Bots join rooms only with consent. Open the bot's **add link** (its owner shares it) and choose a room where you
may manage the room. The consent screen shows who owns the bot and the permissions it asks for. The bot then joins
with a role of exactly the permissions you confirmed, and never more than you have yourself.

<Screenshot scene="consent-desktop" />

**Room settings → Applications** lists the installed bots. Removing one takes it out of the room and deletes its
role. If the owner of a bot is banned, their bots go too.

## No-bots zones

Bot tokens are a single secret, without a second factor. For sensitive places, a room, a category or a single
channel can be marked **No bots**: bots can then neither see nor join it, whatever their roles. New rooms keep their
encrypted channels closed to bots unless a channel explicitly allows them.

## Commands

Bots add their own slash commands, which appear in the command list when you type `/`. In encrypted
conversations, what you type after the command is encrypted too.

## Webhooks

A **webhook** is a secret address that another service, such as a build server or a monitoring tool, uses to post
messages into a channel. Room admins create them under **Room settings → Webhooks**. Posts from a webhook carry a
badge so you can tell them apart.

Webhooks can post into encrypted channels as well, without being able to read them: the post is sealed with a key
that only the channel's members can open.

## Writing your own bot

Anyone can create a bot under **Settings → Applications**. The developer pages describe the
[bot runtime](/docs/developers/sdk/bot/) and the [protocol for applications](/docs/developers/protocol/applications/).
