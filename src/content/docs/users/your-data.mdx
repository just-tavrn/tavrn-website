Your account and what belongs to it live on your home node. You can see what it stores, download it and delete
your account yourself, at any time.

## See and download your data

Open the account menu (your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar) and choose **Legal & privacy → My data**.
**Show summary** lists what your home node stores about you: your profile, devices, rooms, applications with
access, the other nodes your data was shared with through sign-ins and conversations, and how long the node keeps
what. **Download my data** saves everything as a file.

<Screenshot scene="settings-data-desktop" />

The contents of encrypted conversations are not part of the download, because the node cannot read them.

## Delete your account

Under **Legal & privacy → Delete account…** you delete your account on your home node right away and are signed
out everywhere. Your profile, devices and keys, sessions, backups, friends and applications are always deleted.
Your messages stay visible as "Deleted user", without your name or picture, unless you tick **Also delete my messages
and files**.

- If you still own rooms, hand each one to another member or delete it first.
- The node admin can undo a mistaken deletion for a while (30 days on a node with the default setting). After that
  your username becomes free again.
- Other nodes are asked to delete your data as well; they decide themselves.

## The legal pages of a node

Every node can publish an **imprint**, a **privacy policy** and **terms of service**. You find them under **Legal &
privacy** in the account menu and on the sign-in page. When the terms change, the app asks you to read and accept
the new version.

<Screenshot scene="settings-legal-desktop" />

If you come across content that is illegal or breaks the terms, a node can offer a public notice form. Its address
is in the node's legal pages; for a single message you can also use **Report** in its menu.

## Your date of birth

Some nodes ask for your date of birth to enforce a minimum age and rooms for adults. It is stored only on your home
node and never shown to anyone. Other nodes only learn whether you are 18 or older.
