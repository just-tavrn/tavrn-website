A **room** is a community: a gaming group, a club, a team. It lives on one node and has members, roles and
channels, grouped into **categories**. Your rooms are listed in the narrow bar on the far left, the room rail;
rooms of other nodes appear there under their domain. You can group rooms into folders: right-click the empty rail
and choose **New folder…**, or drop one room onto another. A click on a folder opens and closes it; a closed folder
shows small icons of its rooms and adds up their unread messages.

A green speaker on a room's icon means somebody is in one of its voice channels; while somebody shares a screen it
turns into a monitor. Rest the pointer on a room (or press and hold it on a phone) to see its card: the members, the
description, what is new and who is in which voice channel.

## Kinds of channels

| Channel | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Text | Conversations with messages, replies, reactions and threads |
| Voice | Talking, video and screen sharing; click it to join |
| Announcements | News from the moderators; everybody else reacts or replies in a thread |
| Forum | Posts with a title and tags, sorted by latest activity or newest posts |
| Files | A shared folder tree with uploads, folders and previews |

A small lock next to each channel shows its encryption. A closed lock means end-to-end encrypted, which is the
default and always the case for voice channels. An open lock in a warning colour means the channel is not
end-to-end encrypted, and the channel says so in a banner. Some rooms and channels are marked **18+** and are only
open to adults.

Two more marks tell who may use a channel. A group with a slash marks a **private** channel: only some roles may see
it (or, on a voice channel, join it). A no-entry sign means the channel is **restricted for you**: you can see it but
not join it, or only read in it; its name is dimmed, and the channel explains the rest when you open it. Room owners
can check what each role sees with [View room as this role](/docs/users/moderation/#viewing-the-room-as-a-role).

## Joining a room

Most rooms are joined with an **invite link** that a member shares with you. Open it, read the room's rules if it
has any, and choose **Join**. Invite links can expire after an hour, a day, seven days or thirty days, or never,
and can be limited to a number of uses.

Someone can also invite you directly by your address. Such invitations wait in the invite list on your home view
until you accept them.

## Discover

**Discover**, the compass in the room rail, lists the public rooms and nodes your node knows about, across the
whole network. Every card shows the domain of the node that hosts the room, and you can filter by name and size.
Choose **Join** to become a member; for a room on another node, your home node vouches for you, and you keep using
your own account.

Room owners make their room appear in Discover under **Room settings → Overview → Listed in the directory**.

## Creating a room

Choose **+** in the room rail to create a room, if your node allows you to. A new room starts with a text channel,
a voice channel and a welcome channel. Add channels with **Create channel**, and see
[moderation for room owners](/docs/users/moderation/) for roles and permissions.
